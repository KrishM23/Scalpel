From association gap to edited weights.

Scalpel is a bias operations platform: measure how a model leans, locate the circuit, cut it with rank-one surgery, and prove the result.

  • 03

  • 04
    Prove Every job produces before/after metrics, retention scores, and a shareable HTML/JSON audit report your compliance team can file.
Why not fine-tune

Edit the circuit. Keep the model.

Fine-tuning for fairness is slow, expensive, and hard to audit. Scalpel isolates the latent direction responsible for the bias and removes it with a surgical weight edit.

You leave with a versioned artifact — not a hope that prompts will hold in production.
