AI Safety · Model Editing

Find and permanently remove bias from AI models. Without retraining.

Scalpel is a bias-remediation platform for foundation models. It locates the exact internal pathways that carry a bias — down to individual attention heads — and severs them with deterministic, closed-form weight edits. Seconds of compute, not weeks of retraining. Every operation ships with a compliance-grade audit report.

STEP 01 — MEASURE

Quantify the bias with contrastive probes

Scalpel runs the model over minimally-contrastive prompt pairs — "a photo of a man" vs "a photo of a woman", crossed with dozens of templates — and measures how strongly concepts like professions associate with each group, using the peer-reviewed WEAT methodology. This produces a defensible, reproducible baseline: stock CLIP associates "engineer" with men at an effect size of 1.71.

STEP 02 — LOCATE

Isolate the circuit that carries it

Using mechanistic interpretability, Scalpel captures the internal write of every attention head and MLP block, and attributes exactly how much each one transports the bias signal. Because the prompt pairs differ only in the group attribute, everything unrelated cancels out. The result: a ranked bias circuit — typically ~10 of 150+ components — responsible for the behavior.

STEP 03 — CUT

Sever it with closed-form weight surgery

Each circuit component receives a rank-k orthogonal projection — W′ = W − α·Vᵀ(VW) — that makes it mathematically incapable of writing into the bias subspace, for every possible input, forever. No gradients, no fine-tuning, bit-for-bit reproducible. Calibration tunes α so the model lands on neutral, never overcorrected. Everything the component computes outside the bias subspace is untouched.

STEP 04 — PROVE

Audit, document, ship

The pipeline re-measures everything post-surgery: bias metrics before and after, plus commercial-performance retention on neutral prompts (embedding fidelity and ranking geometry). You get the edited model file (the internal numbers — “weights” — that make concepts lean less toward one group), a machine-readable audit, and a printable compliance report — the trail regulators and procurement ask for.

Why teams choose surgery over retraining

01

Seconds, not weeks

A full measure–locate–cut–prove cycle runs in seconds on CPU. Retraining pipelines take weeks, GPUs, and ML staff — and still can't say what changed.

02

Surgical by construction

Edits remove k dimensions out of hundreds, only in components that carry the bias. 99.9% of model behavior is provably untouched — retention is measured, not hoped for.

03

Compliance-native

Deterministic edits + full audit trail per operation. Built for the EU AI Act era: evidence of measurement, remediation, and verification in one artifact.

04

Grounded in research

Built on published methods — WEAT (Caliskan et al.), diff-in-means concept probes, concept erasure (LEACE), directional ablation — engineered into one pipeline.

05

Drop-in output

Edited weights load with the same from_pretrained call as the original. No serving changes, no new dependencies, no lock-in.

06

Neutral, not lobotomized

Calibrated surgery targets zero measured bias rather than maximal erasure — avoiding the overcorrection failure mode naive debiasing methods hit.

Developers

two calls to your first debiased model — full OpenAPI reference at /docs

1 · Queue a surgery

curl 
curl -X POST https://api.scalpel.ai/v1/edit-jobs \
  -H "X-API-Key: $SCALPEL_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "model_id": "openai/clip-vit-base-patch32",
    "bias": "gender_profession",
    "mode": "edit",
    "options": { "num_directions": 4, "calibrate": true },
    "webhook_url": "https://yourapp.com/hooks/scalpel"
  }'
# → 202 { "id": "job_9f2ce81a44d0…", "status": "queued", … }

2 · Fetch results

curl 
# status + summary metrics
curl https://api.scalpel.ai/v1/edit-jobs/$JOB_ID -H "X-API-Key: $SCALPEL_KEY"

# full audit report (JSON) · printable compliance report (HTML)
curl https://api.scalpel.ai/v1/edit-jobs/$JOB_ID/report -H "X-API-Key: $SCALPEL_KEY"
curl https://api.scalpel.ai/v1/edit-jobs/$JOB_ID/report.html -H "X-API-Key: $SCALPEL_KEY"

# edited weights — drop-in replacement
curl -o model.zip https://api.scalpel.ai/v1/edit-jobs/$JOB_ID/artifact -H "X-API-Key: $SCALPEL_KEY"
